Initial screening for Neuromuscular Genetic Disorder (NMGD)

Patient Profile

NMphenoscore – Screening Questions

1. Do you have difficulty walking, running, climbing stairs, or maintaining balance?

2. Do you have trouble rising from the floor or getting up from a chair?

3. Do you feel muscle weakness, stiffness, wasting, or involuntary muscle movements?

4. Do muscle problems interfere with daily activities or swallowing/chewing?

5. Have you experienced breathing problems, fainting, chest pain, or symptoms with exertion?

6. Do you have persistent joint pain, tingling, or difficulty with hearing, speech, or vision?

7. Do you have digestive problems, bladder issues, or significant sleep disturbances?
